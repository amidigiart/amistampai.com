Four steps. No intermediary. Your keys, your stamp.

Generate keys

ECDSA P-256 key pair generated in your browser via Web Crypto API. Private key never leaves your device.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What happens if I lose my private key?

    Your signing key lives in this browser. If you clear site data or switch devices, a new key is created. Your existing stamps remain verifiable: each proof file carries its public key.

    How long does stamping take?

    Under 5 seconds for local stamping. Blockchain anchoring (Pro tier) takes 30-60 seconds depending on network congestion.

    Can I stamp AI-generated content?

    Yes. Stamp the human intention behind the prompt. This provides EU AI Act transparency and proves human oversight.

    Is my content private?

    Yes. All cryptographic operations run in your browser. We never see your content, only the hash you choose to anchor.

    What if AmiStampAI shuts down?

    Your stamps are on the Tezos blockchain and verifiable with standard cryptographic tools. You don't depend on us to verify.
