How it works

Four steps. No intermediary. Your keys, your stamp.

🔑

Generate keys

ECDSA P-256 key pair generated in your browser via Web Crypto API. Private key never leaves your device.

✍️

Declare & sign

Write your content, its meaning, and your intention. All three are bundled and signed with your private key.

🔗

Anchor to blockchain

SHA-256 hashes of the document and signature are recorded on Tezos. Immutable, public, verifiable.

✅

Verify anytime

Anyone with the public key can verify the signature. Anyone can check the blockchain proof. Zero trust required.

Privacy Policy

Data Processing

AmiStampAI performs all cryptographic operations (key generation, signing, hashing) locally in your browser using the Web Crypto API. Your private keys never leave your device.

What We Store

If you choose to persist a stamped document to Firebase, we store: the document hash, the meaning declaration, the intention declaration, the signature, and the timestamp. We do not store the original content unless you explicitly include it.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies for session persistence and cookie consent state. No tracking, no analytics, no third-party cookies.

AI Act Transparency Notice

Classification

AmiStampAI is a cryptographic tool. It does not make decisions, generate content, or classify data using AI. It uses standard cryptographic algorithms (ECDSA, SHA-256) and blockchain technology.

When Used with AI Content

When AmiStampAI is used to stamp AI-generated content, it provides traceability: who requested the content, what was the declared intention, and when was it created. This supports EU AI Act transparency requirements.
